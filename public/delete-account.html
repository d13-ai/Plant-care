Delete your PlantParlour account

From the app, or by email if you can't get in

In the app

  1. Open PlantParlour (the Android app, or plantparlour.org in a browser) and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Choose Delete account, type the word it asks for, and confirm.

It happens straight away. There is no waiting period and nothing to cancel.

If you can't sign in

Email bondcreativestudios@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete it and reply to confirm.

Deleting some of your data, and keeping your account

You don't have to delete your account to remove things from it. In PlantParlour:

  • A care entry (a watering, a note, an issue): open the plant and tap × beside the entry in its history.
  • A plant and everything recorded for it, including its photos, notes and care history: open the plant and choose Remove plant.
  • A published tag: open the plant and choose Unpublish. The link stops working straight away.
  • A single photo, keeping the plant: email bondcreativestudios@gmail.com from your account's address and tell us which plant and photo, and we'll remove it.

Removals reach our servers when your app next syncs, which is usually straight away. Photos are deleted outright. A removed plant or care entry is marked as deleted and hidden everywhere, and kept only as that marker so your other phones and computers know to remove it too; it is erased completely if you delete your account.

What is deleted when you delete your account

  • Your account and sign-in details.
  • Every plant, photo, note and care entry.
  • Published plant tags and your conservatory page.
  • The copy of your greenhouse on the phone or browser you deleted from.

What is kept

  • A daily total of what the AI cost us, with nothing that identifies you.
  • Any bug report or AI-answer report you sent, with your account removed from it.

Our hosting provider's automatic backups age out on their own schedule. Nothing is kept for any other reason.
