About this game

Parlour Games is a small puzzle corner run by PlantParlour. Two games so far: Trickle, where you turn pipes until the water reaches every plant, and Windowsill, where you arrange plants on a stepped shelf so each gets the light it wants.

The house rules apply to everything here: no timers, no scores, nothing to lose. They are the constraint rather than the decoration. Each game has one board a day that is the same for everyone, and a single streak runs across the whole corner — playing any of them today keeps it alive.

Both are free, need no account, run in any browser on any phone or computer, and keep playing once they have loaded even if the connection goes.

Questions people ask

What are Parlour Games?

Two free puzzles that run in a browser — Trickle, a pipe-rotation puzzle, and Windowsill, a light-and-shade placement puzzle. They are made by PlantParlour, a plant care app, as somewhere to sit for a minute between other things.

Are they free? Do I need an account?

Both are free with no ads and nothing to unlock, and neither needs an account. Signing in to PlantParlour only saves your counts and streak across devices.

Why are there no timers or scores?

Because a game that punishes you is a game people quit, and the whole point is the coming back. Nothing here can be lost or failed; the only numbers are there for interest.

Is there a daily puzzle?

Yes — each game has one board a day, the same for everyone in the world that day. Playing either of them keeps your streak going, because the streak is shared across the whole corner rather than kept per game.
