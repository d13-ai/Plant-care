About this game

Elbow Room is a logic puzzle about giving plants space. The plot is divided into coloured beds; plant one in every bed, one in every row and one in every column, and keep every plant clear of the others — not side by side, and not corner to corner. Leaves that touch fight for light and trap damp, which is how mildew spreads along a windowsill.

Some beds hold a plant that bends a rule the way the real one would. A cactus doesn't mind company and may touch its neighbours. A fern keeps out of the sun patches; a succulent needs to be in one. A climber needs a trellis to grow up.

There is no timer, no score and no lives. Every board has exactly one answer and can be worked out by reasoning alone — never by guessing — and if you get stuck, Nudge points at the next thing that can be worked out and says why. Elbow Room is made by PlantParlour; the plant care library has the real light and spacing each houseplant wants.

Questions people ask

How do you play Elbow Room?

Place one plant in every coloured bed, so that every row and every column also has exactly one, and no two plants touch — not even diagonally. Some beds hold a plant that bends a rule: a cactus may touch its neighbours, a fern must stay out of the sun, a succulent must be in it, and a climber needs a trellis.

Is Elbow Room like Queens or Star Battle?

Yes — it is the same family of logic puzzle, with a plant keeper's reason for the rule and four plants that bend it. Every board has exactly one answer and can be finished by reasoning alone, without guessing.

Is Elbow Room free?

Yes. No payment, no account, no advertising and nothing to unlock. It is made by PlantParlour, a plant care app.

Is there a timer, a score or lives?

No. There is no clock, no points and no lives to lose. A plant in the wrong place is shown, not punished, and you can change your mind as often as you like.

What if I get stuck?

Press Nudge. It points at the next thing that can be worked out and says why, without placing anything for you.

What is the daily board?

One board a day, generated from the date, so everyone gets the same puzzle on the same day — with a different twist each day. Playing it keeps your Parlour Games streak alive, and the streak is shared across all the games.
