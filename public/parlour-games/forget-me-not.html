About this game

Forget-Me-Not is the old card game Pairs, played with houseplants. Every plant on the table is there twice, face down. Turn two cards at a time: the same plant twice stays face up, and anything else waits for your next tap before it turns back. Find every pair and the table is clear.

There is no timer and nothing to lose. A pair that doesn't match stays showing until you tap again, so you can look for as long as you like, and the only number — how many turns it took — is shown when you finish, not counted down while you play. Every pair you find comes with one line of real plant care, and most link to the plant's page in the PlantParlour care library. It is made by PlantParlour.

Questions people ask

How do you play Forget-Me-Not?

Turn two cards over at a time. If they show the same plant they stay face up; if not, they wait until you tap again and then turn back. Find every pair.

Is there a timer?

No. There is no clock, and a pair that doesn't match stays showing until you tap again, so you can look for as long as you like. Nothing can be lost.

Is Forget-Me-Not free?

Yes. No payment, no account, no advertising and nothing to unlock. It is made by PlantParlour, a plant care app.

What if I can't remember where a card was?

Press Nudge. If you have already seen both cards of a pair, it shows you where they are. It never shows you a card you haven't turned.

What is the daily table?

One table a day, dealt from the date, so everyone gets the same cards in the same places. Playing it keeps your Parlour Games streak alive, and the streak is shared across all the games.

Are the plant facts true?

Yes. Each pair you find comes with one line of real care, and most link to the plant's page in the PlantParlour care library.
