About this game

Windowsill is a placement puzzle about light. The shelf is stepped, so some places get full sun and others sit in shade, and every plant in the tray wants a particular amount. A plant that is too tall for where it stands will shade whatever is behind it, which is the part that makes it a puzzle rather than a sorting exercise.

There is no timer, no score and nothing to lose. Put a plant down, take it back, try the other order — the board waits. Every board is dealt from an arrangement that already works, so there is always a solution, and the difficulty is measured rather than guessed.

It runs in any browser on any phone or computer, works with touch or a keyboard, plays offline once loaded, and needs no account. Windowsill is made by PlantParlour; if you want the real version of what it is about, the plant care library has the light each houseplant actually wants.

Questions people ask

How do you play Windowsill?

Each place on the shelf gets a certain amount of light, shown as dots — three dots is full sun. Each plant wants a certain amount, and a taller plant shades what sits behind it. Put every plant where the light it gets matches the light it wants, taking the shading into account, and the board is solved.

Is Windowsill free?

Yes. No payment, no account, no advertising and nothing to unlock. It is made by PlantParlour, a plant care app.

Is there a timer or a score?

No. There is no clock, no points and no way to lose. You can put a plant down, pick it up again and change your mind as often as you like.

Can a Windowsill board be impossible?

No. Every board is dealt from an arrangement that already works and then measured for difficulty, so a solution always exists.

What is the daily board?

One board a day, generated from the date, so everyone gets the same puzzle on the same day. Playing it keeps your Parlour Games streak alive — and the streak is shared across all the games, so any one of them counts.

Does Windowsill teach you anything about real plants?

Loosely. The idea it is built on is true: houseplants have a light level they want, too much scorches and too little makes them stretch, and a big plant in front of a small one will shade it out. The numbers in the puzzle are a game, not a care guide — for the real thing, see the plant care library.
